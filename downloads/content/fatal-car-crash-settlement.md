## Fatal Car Crash Settlements: What to Expect in your first meeting.

No doubt you will be feeling anger, frustration, and incredibly emotional throughout this meeting, it is important for your lawyer to understand the facts whilst supporting you and your family through this incredibly painful process. When you lose a child, spouse, or parent in a [fatal car accident](https://phxinjurylaw.com/fatal-car-accident/), the laws surrounding this tragedy are known as wrongful death laws. 

[Wrongful death laws are in place in the state of Arizona](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/fatal-car-accident-settlement/) to protect and represent people just like you and your family who have experienced the devastation of death as a direct result of someone else’s negligence or carelessness.

## Fatal Car Crash Settlements: Who can file a wrongful death claim and who can accept the settlement.

If your [spouse](https://phxinjurylaw.com/wrongful-death-claim-in-phoenix/), [child](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/wrongful-death-of-an-unborn-child-fetus/), or a direct family member has been involved in an accident then you can file a wrongful death claim on their behalf. Sadly, if you’re a friend or neighbor and not related, you won’t be able to file a case for their accident. Your attorney will discuss the nature and circumstances of the accident with you initially to help establish and build your case.

You may have questions about how the [at fault driver will be held responsible](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/fatal-drunk-driving/), who all the responsible parties are and [what type of punishment, if any, they may receive](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/arizona-punitive-damages-wrongful-death/). What is the timeline for a case, what does the [criminal process look like](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/how-to-claim-damages-after-a-car-accident/)? We can answer these questions for you and more.
