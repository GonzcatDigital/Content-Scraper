## Medical Treatment After Hit and run Accidents
Firstly, suppose you’ve been involved in a hit-and-run accident. In that case, it’s really important to get medical treatment as soon as possible, either by the roadside from emergency services or visiting the ER as soon as you can. If you’ve been lucky enough to have minimal injuries or feel ok even after an accident, we’d always advise you to visit a doctor, even if it’s just for a once-over. 

Getting a [medical professional to check you’re ok](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/doctor-after-a-car-accident/) from a medical point of view after a hit-and-run is important when building a legal case, as some injuries can be undetected or even painless. If you’ve had an accident at high-speed, you may have whiplash or other soft tissue damage that doesn’t yet cause pain. A professional accident doctor will be able to examine you properly and make sure you get the right treatment.
