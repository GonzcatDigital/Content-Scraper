## Understanding Hit and Run Accidents

### What Constitutes a Hit and Run in Arizona?

A hit-and-run occurs when a driver causes damage to a vehicle, person, property, or even an animal and then flees the scene without reporting the incident or leaving any contact information. This cowardly act can leave victims feeling cheated, violated, and financially burdened. Hit-and-run is a devastating road accident case with far-reaching consequences. You could be injured, minor or severely, or in the worst case scenario, the accident could be fatal. This situation is typical in a usual crash. Still, in a hit and run, it is aggravated by the party responsible for fleeing the scene, evading responsibility. That is why it is necessary to have the assistance of a hit and run lawyer from Phoenix Accident and Injury Law Firm to ensure that justice is done.

### Why Drivers Flee Accident Scenes

According to our hit and run attorney, the most common reasons for drivers to flee the scene are:  
Lack of insurance – uninsured or underinsured drivers  
Suspended or unlicensed driver’s license  
The driver has too many tickets  
Driver may not be allowed to use the vehicle they are driving  
Driving under the influence of drugs and/or alcohol  
On probation for other criminal conduct, including holding an arrest warrant  
Driving a company car and not wanting to suffer consequences at work  
When the law catches up to hit and run drivers, victims can hold them accountable for their selfish and reckless actions with the help of a hit and run lawyer.

## Legal Responsibilities After a Hit and Run

In Arizona, the law is explicit: if you are involved in a hit and run accident resulting in injury or death, you must remain at the scene. Fulfill several legal requirements, such as providing your name, address, and other pertinent information. You are also obligated to render aid to anyone injured in the crash. Failing to comply can result in serious felony charges. Despite these legal obligations, many drivers choose to flee the scene, neglecting both the law and the well-being of those affected.

According to recent [studies](https://aaafoundation.org/hit-and-run-crashes-prevalence-contributing-factors-and-countermeasures/), there is a hit and run accident in the United States every minute, and approximately 5% of all [auto fatalities](https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/Publication/813458) are caused by hit and run drivers. ### Your Immediate Steps Following an Accident

**DON’T CHASE**. If you do, you’ll also be leaving the scene of the crash. You’re not a police officer. Chasing a hit-and-run driver can be dangerous.  
**Call the police**. There is a process they must follow after a crash. They will take your statement and record evidence.  
**See your doctor as needed**. Even if you don’t feel sick immediately after the accident, you may feel the effects for days to come.  
**Call a hit and run lawyer**. The authorities may never catch the hit-and-run driver, but you may still be able to file an insurance claim. Sometimes, even when the other driver is caught, they may not even have insurance. An experienced hit-and-run lawyer will be able to guide you, protect your best interests, and take care of the insurance company and paperwork.  
**Call your insurance provider**. If the other driver cannot be located, or if they are located but do not have insurance, your insurance policy should cover the damage. How this is handled depends on your policy. Whatever happens, remember that your insurance company makes money by paying out as little as possible. Having an attorney means you can direct the insurer to speak to your attorney and avoid having to talk to them about it.
