## Electronic Device Regulations for Semi-Trucks Safety

Some of the device regulations for semi-trucks safety that have been introduced are electronic logging systems and control systems, both designed to help ensure safety at all times the truck is out on the road.

-   Electronic logging devices empower drivers to have their hours accurately and instantly recorded and updated, meaning employers can keep on top of breaks and ensure their drivers are safe when they’re out on the road
-   [Electronic stability control systems for heavier vehicles over 11,793kgs.](http://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/fmvss/136_ESC_Hvy_Veh_PRIA.pdf) These devices are designed to significantly reduce the chances of the truck either under or oversteering and potentially rolling over.

In 2012, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, NHTSA, stated that they expected 26% of new truck tractors and 80% of new buses to be equipped with ESC systems, complying with device regulations for semi-trucks safety. These systems were estimated to reduce the occurrence of untripped rollover crashes by 40 to 56% and loss-of-control crashes by 14%. Such device regulations for semi-trucks safety aim to enhance the overall safety of these vehicles and mitigate the risks associated with accidents.
