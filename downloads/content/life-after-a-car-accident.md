## Life After a Car Accident: Ongoing Injuries

If you’ve been involved in an accident that has resulted in you being injured, no matter how short-term or even life-changing, every injury feels amplified by the fact your accident could have been prevented. We previously covered living with injuries after an accident and giving yourself time to heal. The first thing to do is seek medical advice about any [pains, aches,](https://phxinjurylaw.com/practice-areas/chronic-pain-after-car-accident/) and even [headaches](https://phxinjurylaw.com/practice-areas/car-accident-brain-injury/) after an accident, as any new pains could indicate further health issues.

When you file your accident case, your attorney will ask about injuries and other medical conditions that have occurred as a direct result of being involved in your accident. Understanding how your life has changed will help your accident attorney agree to a settlement that truly reflects the loss you’ve endured and give a complete picture of your life after a car accident. 

## Life After a Car Accident: Handling Repairs of Your Vehicle.

Knowing your rights when navigating insurance companies is another important element after an accident, as you don’t have to wait for an insurer to decide when your vehicle is fixed. If your vehicle is deemed repair-worthy by a mechanic, you’re within your rights to have it repaired as soon as possible. If you can, you can even [fix your car](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/fix-car-with-insurance-money/) or motorbike yourself!

## Having the first refusal of your financial settlement.

Another important part of being involved in an accident is knowing your rights regarding financial settlements- and learning when you can turn them down! Insurers often offer a lump sum of money after an accident in the hope you’ll accept and use it to fix your car and pay for medical bills, but knowing you can turn it down means you’ll be more likely to receive a better settlement with a legal representative.
