## Understanding Brain Injuries

Traumatic brain injuries are frequent in most car-related accidents and are one cause most people die in the aftermath of car collisions. Our brain injury lawyers understand these types of injuries because the brain is one of the most delicate parts of the human body. Here are the common causes of brain injuries: 

-   Falls
-   Vehicle accidents
-   Physical altercations
-   Assaults 
-   Sports

Despite the fact these are not the only way to be brain injured, these are the most frequent traumatic ones. For our traumatic brain injury lawyers, it is crucial to know the cause since nature itself will explain the outcome of the damage, and it will play an essential role in your claim. Brain injuries usually have similar symptoms, and if you or your loved ones are involved in a traumatic accident, you can recognize them easily. Here are some of the most common: 

-   Loss of consciousness for more than 2 min
-   Confusion
-   Slurred speech
-   Lack of coordination
-   Headache
-   Numbness in one side of the body or the whole body
-   Weakness in general
-   Vomiting
-   Lack of pupillary response 

## Types of Brain Injury Cases We Handle

Not all brain injuries are the same and won’t evolve in the same direction. Depending on the cause and the factors involved in the brain injury, you or your loved ones will have a mild, moderate, or severe injury that requires more or less medical assistance. Our brain injury lawyers will support you on your way to recovery and obtain the compensation you deserve.

Depending on your symptoms, a brain injury can be a mild concussion, where you experience a terrible time after an accident. Moderate brain injury can involve dizziness, difficulties with thinking, attention, memory, organizing, concentration, and word-finding problems, and irritability. Severe brain injuries include broken bones and even the twisting of the brain inside the skull. Our TBI lawyers will support you during these times of need and help you during the legal process.
