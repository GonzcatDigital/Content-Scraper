## Open Containers in Arizona

[Arizona law prohibits](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/4/00251.htm) anyone from consuming or possessing an open container of alcohol inside motor vehicles. The law not only applies to drivers but also applies to open containers in the possession of passengers in motor vehicles. The passenger compartment for purposes of this law includes unlocked compartments inside the vehicle. It does not extend to the trunk or the area behind the back seats. Essentially, the law is available to prohibit open containers of alcohol anywhere inside a vehicle that may be within reach of the driver.

In many states, open containers can even include an empty bottle or can that once held alcohol so long as some alcohol remains in the container. A violation does not extend to the smell of alcohol. But even an empty bottle may be sufficient for a police officer to conduct [a DUI investigation.](https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/free-books/beat-ticket-book/chapter7-10.html) As Arizona has some of the strictest DUI laws in the country, an Arizona Open Container Laws violation may have consequences beyond the class-2 misdemeanor for that charge alone. Once a police officer is conducting a DUI investigation, he or she merely needs sufficient evidence of the driver being [impaired to the slightest degree](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/01381.htm) to charge him or her with a DUI.
