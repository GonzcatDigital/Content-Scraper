## What Causes Wrong Way drivers?

As with all traffic accidents, the reasons behind a wrong way accident can vary in each case but many of these accidents are caused by:

-    Distracted driving
-   Driving on unknown highways or roads
-   Driving under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or even medication 

[Wrong way driving accidents](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/distracted-driving-injury-awareness-month/) seem to be largely avoidable to road users as there are visual signs to help us stay in our lanes but what happens if drivers miss those signs or aren’t concentrating? It might seem like a very small percentage of traffic accidents but surely wrong way accidents are completely avoidable!

## What lane do wrong way drivers tend to drive down?

There is a pattern of wrong way drivers ending up in the wrong lane, which is nearly always the left lane! In fact, according to The National Transportation Safety Board, 7 out of 9 wrong way accidents happened because a driver was going the wrong way in the left-hand lane. This is a surprisingly high number of drivers finding themselves head-on with the flow of traffic and there is one state in the US that sees a disproportionately high number of wrong-way drivers 

## How Many Wrong Way Driver Accidents Happen In Arizona

According to figures from [Gutier](https://eu.azcentral.com/story/news/local/arizona-traffic/2019/08/02/arizona-16-wrong-way-driver-fatalities-through-july-2019/1894369001/), in 2019 the state of Arizona saw 1,088 wrong way driving accidents alone, 100 more accidents than the previous year. So why are drivers in Arizona finding themselves down the wrong way? One of the key factors for these accidents happening is the time of day- wrong way accidents typically happen after 6 pm at night. It’s important to be aware of wrong way drivers and if you’re faced with a vehicle coming at you the wrong way, it’s best to pull over as soon as possible to avoid an accident.
