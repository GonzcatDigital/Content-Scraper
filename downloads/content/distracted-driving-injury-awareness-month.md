### Signs of Distracted Driving: Distracted Driving Injury Awareness Month

A distracted driver is one that is not paying attention to the road. People will talk on the phone, look for directions, and even check their email. Distracted driving also occurs when a parent is trying to control screaming children, the radio is too loud, or the person [looks down at a map to figure out where they are](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/regulating-map-apps-in-arizona/). Signs of distracted driving include:

-   swerving over the yellow line
-   stopping abruptly
-   inconsistent speeds

During distracted driving injury awareness month, it is essential to remain vigilant and observe any signs of a driver who is not focused on the road. These signs can be similar to those exhibited by an intoxicated driver, with both displaying behaviors such as looking down instead of maintaining proper attention. However, it is worth noting that a drunk driver may exhibit more pronounced swerving compared to a distracted driver. By being aware of these indicators during distracted driving injury awareness month, we can help identify and address potential dangers on the road, promoting a safer driving environment for everyone.

## Tips for Avoiding Distracted Driving During Distracted Driving Injury Awareness Month

During distracted driving injury awareness month, it is vital to understand how to effectively avoid distracted driving. One key measure is to refrain from using mobile media while operating a vehicle, ensuring your full attention is on the road. Additionally, consider turning off the car radio if it proves to be a source of distraction. Recognizing the importance of maintaining focus, particularly when feeling tired or experiencing difficulty concentrating, it is crucial to pull over and take a break.

This simple yet often overlooked advice can significantly reduce the risk of being involved in a serious car accident, fostering a safer environment during distracted driving injury awareness month.
