### Common Examples of Minor Car Accidents  

Minor car accidents typically involve:

-   Fender-benders: A slight collision between two vehicles.
-   Broken lights: Damage to headlights, taillights, or turn signals.
-   Tire damage: Punctures, blowouts, or other tire-related issues.
-   Windshield damage: Cracks or breaks in the windshield.
-   Cosmetic damage: Small dents, dings, or scratches.
-   Side mirror damage: Broken or damaged side mirrors.

## Legal Considerations for Minor Car Accidents 

### When and How to Report a Minor Car Accident 

In Arizona, it’s crucial to report any car accident that results in injuries or property damage. Promptly reporting the collision can protect your legal rights and take the necessary steps to investigate the incident.

To report a car accident, follow these steps:

1.  **Call 911:** Immediately contact emergency services to inform them of the accident.
2.  **Provide details:** Be prepared to give your location, the number of vehicles involved, and any injuries.
3.  **Cooperate with law enforcement:** Follow the instructions of the responding officers and provide any requested information.

### The Importance of Documenting Everything 

A police report is a vital piece of evidence in car accident cases. It is used to:

-   **Support insurance claims:** Provide documentation to your insurance company when seeking compensation for damages.
-   **Build a strong case:** Serve as evidence in a lawsuit against a negligent driver.
