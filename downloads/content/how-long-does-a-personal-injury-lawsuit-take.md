## Watch out for the ‘settlement’ game

Often, insurance companies play ‘the settlement game’ as they’re often inundated with accident claims and need to wrap them up quickly to move on. This approach means they’ll often overlook your individual needs of you- the victim of an accident that wasn’t your fault, offering a random amount to help cover your bills.

A personal injury settlement will be sent to you as a lump sum without considering the injuries or any ongoing costs that you’ve endured since your accident.

Don’t accept anything you’re offered if you don’t feel your pain is being considered in the sum they’ve suggested. You’re well within your legal rights to reject a personal injury settlement from the insurance company and pursue a higher amount.
