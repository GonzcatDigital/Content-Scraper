## What Expenses are Recoverable as Potential Damages for victims of DUI accident settlements?

The types of damages listed above, property damage and medical treatment, are two of the most common types of damages that people think of in relation to [car accidents](https://phxinjurylaw.com/locations/chandler-car-accident-lawyers/). There are numerous other expenses that can incur recovery for victims of DUI accident settlements. What many people fail to think about are some of the more intangible damages, such as [lost wages](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/lost-wages-vs-lost-earning-capacity/), diminished employment ability, pain and suffering, and loss of consortium (or affection). These types of damages can cost potentially into the hundreds of thousands of dollars. If a [fatality](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/fatal-drunk-driving/) is involved, the economic costs can be well [over a million dollars per incident](https://azdot.gov/sites/default/files/media/2022/09/2021-Arizona-Motor-Vehicle-Crash-Facts-highlights.pdf) in the state of Arizona.
