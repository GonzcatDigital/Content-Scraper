## Does Arizona Law require you to wear a seatbelt?

Definitely. In Arizona, it is mandated by law that individuals in the front seat of a vehicle capable of carrying 10 or fewer people must wear seatbelts. Acting in accordance with the legal standard of a “reasonable person,” it is your responsibility to comply with this requirement and secure your seatbelt. Furthermore, you are also expected to ensure that your front-seat passengers are properly buckled, and that all passengers under the age of 16 are wearing seatbelts, irrespective of their location within the vehicle, in order to prioritize seatbelts in car accidents.

## Will NOT wearing a seatbelt affect your compensation for injuries?

If you get injuries in a car accident, it’s important to know that Arizona is a _comparative fault_ state. That means if the defense can prove that your injuries are due to your failure to wear a seatbelt in car accident—even if the person who caused the car accident was completely at fault—you may be denied compensation.
