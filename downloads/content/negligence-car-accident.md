## Do you know what it really means to be negligent in Arizona?

In a Negligence Car Accident, the focus of the law is on addressing the harm caused by inattention or carelessness, rather than intent, which is typically required in criminal cases. In essence, it’s an accident, but someone is still responsible, and an Arizona court will advocate for your rights as a victim to receive fair compensation that aligns with the specifics of your accident and the resulting suffering.

[Examples of when someone has acted negligent](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/dui-laws-in-arizona-explained/) and as a result caused an accident, not intentionally but due to their own carelessness include:

-   Driving under the influence of drinks or drugs.
-   Being distracted by a phone or even passenger when driving.
-   Not looking at a junction or ignoring road signs and signals.
-   Pulling out in front of another moving. 

In a Negligence Car Accident case, the attorney will contend that although the incident was unintentional, the plaintiff had a legal obligation to exercise care and caution in order to prevent such accidents. Driving while under the influence of alcohol, for instance, is a behavior that can be easily avoided, highlighting the element of negligence. As both drivers and pedestrians, we all carry the responsibility of ensuring that our actions do not cause harm to others while using Arizona’s roads.
