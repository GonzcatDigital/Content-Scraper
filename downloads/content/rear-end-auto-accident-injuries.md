## The Accident Scene: Rear-End Auto Accident Injuries
The rear-end auto accident happened on January 5, 2016, around 6:45 a.m. in Chandler, Arizona. A.V. was driving her 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe westbound on Ray Road in the right lane approaching Price Road. The traffic in front of A.V. began to slow and A.V. applied her brakes almost coming to a full stop when the vehicle behind her collided with the back of her vehicle causing a rear-end auto accident.

The Chandler Police Department arrived on the scene and interviewed A.V. as well as the at-fault driver. The at-fault driver told the Chandler Police that she tried to brake in time, but the wet pavement caused her to collide with A.V.’s vehicle. The police officers on scene cited the at-fault driver for failure to control speed to avoid a collision. A.V. drove her vehicle from the scene and refused medical transport or assistance at the scene.

## Head and Back Injuries: Rear-end Auto Accident Injuries
It was six days after the collision that A.V. saw a [professional doctor, Dr. Cerre,](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/why-you-need-an-auto-accident-doctor/) who diagnosed A.V. with cervical sprain/strain, lumbar sprain/strain, sacrum sprain/strain, right hip sprain/strain, pain in the right knee, and myofascitis. [Dr. Cerr](https://ericcerrenmd.com/)e treated her with a course of chiropractic therapy but due to A.V.’s continued pain, Dr. Cerre then administered six Lidocaine and Marcaine trigger point injections to A.V.’s cervical, thoracic and lumbar regions. This treatment was intended to alleviate her head and back injury pain.

A.V. then had x-rays of her cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine, right hip, and right knee after her initial consultation with Dr. Cerre. A.V.’s X-ray of the cervical spine revealed a loss of normal lordotic curvature of the cervical spine with neck flexion. The X-ray of her right hip showed an irregular sclerotic lesion in the proximal right femur, likely an enchondroma. Her X-rays of thoracic and lumbosacral spines and right knee revealed no significant abnormalities.

### Treatment and Medical Expenses for** **Rear-end Auto Accident Injuries
A.V. continued to experience pain and Dr. Cerre had her submit to an MRI of her cervical spine. Her cervical spine reports revealed a loss of the normal lordotic curvature of the cervical spine with bulging of a disc as well as a shallow posterior disc herniation. As A.V.’s pain persisted, Dr. Cerre referred A.V. to Orange Medical Pain Management where she was diagnosed with cervicalgia, cervical disc disorder of the mid-cervical region, and cervical disc displacement. 

The pain management doctors advised A.V. to undergo cervical epidural steroid injections and trigger point injection therapy and recommended home exercise and stretching exercise programs on a daily basis. A.V. received one treatment from Orange Medical Pain Management and was treated with Dr. Cerre for around six weeks with a [total of 11 visits at a cost of $8,918.90.](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/medical-treatment-after-a-car-accident/)
