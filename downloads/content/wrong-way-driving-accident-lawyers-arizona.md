## Causes of Wrong-Way Driving Accidents

According to Arizona law [(A. R. S. § 28-694)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/00694.htm), any person who drives in the wrong way on a controlled access highway commits wrong way driving, which will result in a violation and receive a civil penalty of $5,000. However, despite the law, some negligent drivers still risk their lives in danger for this reckless decision. Some of the most common causes are: 

-   **Driving under the influence:** it is a common cause of wrong way drivers‘ unlawful actions. Usually, these drivers make a blatant error, like entering a freeway using an exit ramp or simply making a prohibited turn in an intersection or a street. There are more ways intoxicated drivers can cause a collision and injure you or your loved ones. 
-   **Distracted driving:** A cause that will always be relevant among car accidents is drivers who get distracted by talking or texting. Without the proper attention, these negligent drivers can make a wrong turn and enter a one-way street. 
-   **Older drivers:** a high rate of older drivers incur into wrong way driving for medical conditions like dementia or impaired vision that can lead them to make a catastrophic life-changing event for you or your loved ones.
-   **Poorly marked on ramps:** Signs are necessary to let all drivers know when they need to make a turn or stop. However, poorly marked ramps that don’t include signs like “Do Not Enter” or warnings can lead drivers to enter a street or highway in the wrong direction. 
-   **Unfamiliar drivers:** Beginner drivers or those who are unfamiliar with streets and roadways may accidentally cross into opposing traffic. Moreover, they might mistake an off-ramp for an on-ramp or enter a one-way street the wrong way.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Wrong-Way Accidents

### What are the immediate steps to take after a wrong way accident?

1.  Call 911 or make someone do it to get medical attention for any injury you or your loved ones might have. 
2.  If you don’t have any severe injuries, move to a safe location
3.  Wait for the police officer to fill out the report.
4.  Record and photograph the scene from every angle possible
5.  Do not admit fault
6.  Contact an attorney to help you file your claim

### What to do if you see someone is wrong way driving?

If you or your loved ones watch someone opposite the traffic, pull over and slow down; try to move as far as you can from this situation. In any case, you can move to the right or stay in the center of the lane to make a maneuver to move to the left or right if needed. Use your flashlights, horn, and turn on your hazard light to inform other drivers about the situation. Call 911 to inform the situation and explain all the details possible about the wrong way driver car.
