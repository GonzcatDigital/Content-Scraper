## Understanding Left-Turn Accidents in Phoenix

A left turn collision happens when the at-fault driver fails to stop at an intersection and makes the turn carelessly, heading into the oncoming traffic. This incurred as unlawful action when the at-fault driver didn’t respect the right-of-way to other vehicles approaching from the opposite direction. During this life-changing event, our left turn accident lawyers will evaluate your case with all the details to support you or your loved ones. 

Most wrong left turn collisions occur because the at-fault driver ignores the traffic rules stated in Arizona’s left turn law ([A. R. S. § 28-772](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/00772.htm)). This law states that the driver of a vehicle within an intersection must yield the right-of-way to other drivers who are approaching from the opposite direction, avoiding any immediate hazard if one is within the intersection or close to it. 

Despite the fact it is a collision that is relatively frequent and involves a wrongful left turn, there are different types to consider during the aftermath with our left turn accident lawyers. Here are the most common left turn collisions: 

-   **Left turn at an intersection:** This is one of the most common left turn accidents you or your loved ones could be a victim of since any negligent driver can overlook the traffic light or deny the right-of-way. 
-   **Left turn at a four-way stop:** One of the most common ways for an at-fault driver to create an accident for failing to stop at the traffic light. 
-   **Left turn in a U-turn:** It is a usual maneuver most drivers make every day, but it can incur a left turn accident if a negligent driver fails to stop at the red light or is distracted during the turn, causing a collision. 
-   **Left turn into a parking lot:** Some places where left turn accidents happen are parking lots for blocked vision and reckless drivers who exceed the speed limit, creating scenarios for our left turn accident lawyers to be involved and help you navigate the complicated legal process in the aftermath of this situation.
