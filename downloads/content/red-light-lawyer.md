Anything outside this law is considered unlawful, and a red light accident attorney can use this as evidence to support your case.

## Common Causes of Red Light Accidents

The causes at-fault drivers incur into running red lights can differ from one to another accident; however, the most common causes that cause red light accidents are widespread across Arizona. Here are the reasons our red light attorneys usually assist our clients: 

-   **Distracted Driving:** It can be for using a smartphone, changing the radio, talking with a passenger, or any reason that causes one to lose concentration on the road and the traffic light, leading to a collision for running the red light.
-   **Aggressive Driving Behavior:** This category includes behaviors such as speeding, tailgating, violence behind the wheel, road rage, and more that can lead to a red light accident. These types of aggressiveness while driving are against the law and driver education and create dangerous scenarios for collisions.
-   **Impatience:** It is common to see drivers impatient to reach their destination, which can lead to a red light accident because of the impulsive decisions these types of drivers can make. 
-   **Driving Under the Influence**: One of the most common cases our red light lawyers assist with is driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs. This can result in two different unlawful actions and is a usual cause for running a red light at any time of the day.
