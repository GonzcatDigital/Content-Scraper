## Involved In A High-Speed Hit And Run Crash? What To Do First

Firstly, if you’ve been involved in a hit-and-run at any speed, you’ll need to call the police and an ambulance. Getting immediate emergency help will ensure your injuries are handled and your legal case will have witnesses to the accident scene. The emergency services will also have experience handling [hit-and-run accidents](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/young-couple-killed-by-high-speed-hit-and-run-driver-in-south-la/3157142/) and will be able to help you and any passengers stay calm whilst looking for any other witnesses that may have seen what happened.

When someone hits your vehicle and flees the scene it can feel incredibly unjust and frustrating, leaving you feeling far worse than bruised and battered. Being hit at high-speed significantly increases the risk of severe injury too so it’s always best to get medical treatment as soon as possible. It can also be really difficult to prove the speed in an accident case so a doctor will assess injuries and help build a bigger picture from them to help your case.

## What To Do After A High-Speed Hit And Run Accident

If you’re lucky enough to walk away from your accident, we’d always recommend going to ER to be assessed to make sure there’s nothing undetected or more serious. Being hit at any speed is potentially dangerous but being hit at speed can leave far more serious injuries. 

When you’ve been involved in a high-speed hit and run crash, in particular, it can be really tempting to start investigating your own accident and even try to look for the perpetrator but it’s important to let the police handle your case. With the absence of a plaintiff initially, it can feel very frustrating but we’d always recommend starting your legal case anyway and choosing an accident attorney to help handle your case.
