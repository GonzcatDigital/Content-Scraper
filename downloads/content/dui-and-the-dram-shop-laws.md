## DUI Accidents and Dram Shop Laws

## ![discussion of broken bones in a car accident](data:image/svg+xml;nitro-empty-id=MzIxOjU2Nw==-1;base64,PHN2ZyB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzAwIDIyNSIgd2lkdGg9IjMwMCIgaGVpZ2h0PSIyMjUiIHhtbG5zPSJodHRwOi8vd3d3LnczLm9yZy8yMDAwL3N2ZyI+PC9zdmc+)

You may know someone who has been a victim of a DUI accident, or perhaps even experienced yourself which is no surprise as statistics show [over 1 million drivers were arrested for driving under the influence](https://www.cdc.gov/transportationsafety/impaired_driving/impaired-drv_factsheet.html) in 2016 alone. It may seem like an easy thing to avoid but it only takes one large drink on an empty stomach or a drag on marijuana for you or a friend to have an accident as judgment is impaired. If you’re a victim of a DUI accident, through no fault of your own, you will be pleased to learn that in the state of Arizona, dram shop laws will be in place to help protect you further whilst sharing the responsibility of the driver with the bar owner. 

Dram shops have a social responsibility as a licensed venue, to exercise power when it comes to serving their customers- especially those who arrive in a car and expect to leave in the same way. The responsibility of the bar or dram shop owner to stop serving people who have simply had enough and no other way of getting home.

## [DUI victims](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/financial-aftermath-of-a-dui/) and their rights: The Dram Shop Laws

Driving under the influence has huge consequences, as you’ll know, having been a victim of a [DUI accident](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/restitution-for-dui-accident/), but sadly it can be prevented and by enforcing both dram shops and bars to actively keep on top of serving their customers, it’s a step in the right direction to help prevent inevitable accidents that would otherwise happen due to someone’s carelessness.

Proving someone is over the limit with alcohol is something police departments across both Arizona and the rest of America, have a lot of experience with and requesting a road-side breath test is the first step to seeking justice after an accident.
