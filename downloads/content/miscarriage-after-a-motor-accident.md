## Wrongful death: Miscarriage After A Motor Accident

If you’ve recently suffered a [miscarriage due to a motor accident](https://phxinjurylaw.com/practice-areas/miscarriage-after-a-car-accident/), your case could be filed as a [wrongful death claim](https://phxinjurylaw.com/wrongful-death-claim-in-phoenix/), here in the state of Arizona, as changes in our laws mean a fetus will be considered able to live outside the womb. Wrongful death claims can be filed when an accident causes unintentional death whether that is immediately or through sustained injuries. A miscarriage after a motor accident is an example of where the law has been updated to protect an unborn fetus, recognizing them as an individual in the case.

Wrongful death claims recognize negligence or failure to act on the third party’s behalf and hold them accountable for their actions. Having a car accident may seem straightforward when it comes to identifying who is to blame but when a life is ended as a direct result, it becomes a much more complex and emotionally traumatic incident. Whilst there may be a clear individual to blame for the car accident, a strong legal team will then work to represent you and your family as best they can to seek a suitable settlement that truly reflects the loss and ongoing emotional trauma experienced.
