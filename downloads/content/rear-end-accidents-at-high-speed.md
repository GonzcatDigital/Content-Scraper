## What Is a Rear-End Collision?

Rear-end accidents are when a negligent driver reaches you from the back. In these types of accidents, it is common that the at-fault driver is the one who struck you from behind, leaving you the impact in the back of the car. Although the most common car accidents are the ones with the lower rates of fatalities, you can still suffer from different injuries like whiplash and fractures.  
  
One scenario in which these types of collisions happen is in heavy traffic; however, they are not related to only these scenarios since a negligent driver can cause an accident even on an empty road. ## Determining Fault in Rear-End Collisions

Most of the time, proving the liability of the driver who struck you from behind is easy. The rule for these cases is that the driver who spotted the car in front should apply the brakes in time. If rear-end accidents occur without further evidence, these rules will apply, and the driver who struck you from the back will be at fault. In some cases, this will not apply, but the further investigation will be more extended.
