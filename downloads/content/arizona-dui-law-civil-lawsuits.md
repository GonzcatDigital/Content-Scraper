## The DUI Law in Arizona Concerning DUI Drivers
Civil cases look to compensate victims of individuals who break the law for damages that they incurred because of illegal behavior. **A civil court** will defend the victim or person impacted directly by a DUI driver who has already pleaded guilty and acknowledged the illegal behavior and subsequent damages that have happened as a direct result of their actions.

## Civil Court- DUI Law Trials
Civil courts are the issue of damages and the plea; a DUI driver will likely either initially plead guilty or not guilty only to be found guilty of a DUI. Depending on several other factors surrounding the case, such as how drunk the driver was when breathalyzed, the damage to the cars, and the injuries sustained, your legal representative will decide which legal path will best suit your individual case.
