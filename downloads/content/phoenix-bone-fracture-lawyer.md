### Types of Bone Fractures and Their Impact

Fractures and broken bones are common injuries in Phoenix, AZ, often resulting from another party’s negligence. Here are some types of fractures our bone fracture lawyer usually encounters:

  
**Complete fractures**: A classic “broken bone” where the bone snaps into two pieces.

**Comminuted fractures**: A more severe fracture where the bone breaks into three or more pieces.

**Hairline fractures**: Also known as “stress fractures,” these are tiny cracks in the bone.

**Stable fractures**: The bone cracks but does not shift out of place.

**Displaced fractures**: The bone cracks and shifts out of alignment.

**Incomplete fractures**: The bone does not break entirely apart.

**Compound or open fractures**: A severe fracture where the bone punctures the skin.

**Transverse fractures**: A break that occurs horizontally.

**Oblique fractures**: A break that occurs diagonally.

**Simple fractures**: A general term for various types of fractures, including comminuted, impacted, oblique, transverse, and hairline fractures.

**Spiral fractures**: A break that follows a twisting pattern, often caused by slips and falls.

**Greenstick fractures**: A fracture that occurs in a bone that hasn’t fully hardened, causing it to bend and crack but remain in one piece.

**Segmental fractures**: A fracture where a bone breaks into two or more pieces, and one piece is disconnected from the others.

**Any of these fractures can have a significant impact on your life.** A broken bone injury can disrupt your daily routine, limit your mobility, and affect your ability to work or participate in activities you enjoy.
