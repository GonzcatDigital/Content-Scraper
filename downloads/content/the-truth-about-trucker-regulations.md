## Accidents on I-10 and I-17, what’s the deal?

With commercial trucks helping to keep our state stocked up and running every day, driving along the most congested highways in Arizona, it may not come as much of a surprise to learn that these roads see the majority of big truck accidents across the state.

In fact, many of the previous cases our team has represented involved a truck on one of these roads, meaning when it comes to representing your case- we’ve been there before. We know how to tell what trucker regulations were violated. 

As the busiest roads in the state, an experienced attorney will immediately consider other factors that may have impacted your collision such as time of day, whether the truck driver was within their allotted hours and the general maintenance of the truck. Did you know, truck drivers are restricted to 11 hours on the road every time, there could be a chance they were in breach of this when they had an accident.

## Trucker Regulations- Are They Strict Enough?

Whilst federal law in our state insists on applying strict safety trucker regulations such as watching drivers hours and making sure truckers take regular breaks. There are also strict rules about regular maintenance, with employers being solely responsible for regulating their fleet and ensuring they’re all road-safe every month.

Sadly, there are too many companies that skip checking the trucks are fit to be on the road for potential money-saving aims, avoiding having mechanical checks and even replacing tires, as well as other trucker regulations- all to save a few dollars.

Modern living and higher demands have only put more pressure on businesses to deliver goods and produce any time of the day or night, meaning drivers are relied on to work around the clock. In order to help regulate truckers and their employers, the law has tightened trucker regulations in recent years, serving out hefty fines if employers are found in breach.
