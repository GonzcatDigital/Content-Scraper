## Why You Need an Auto Accident Doctor

Another reason your family doctor may refuse to treat you has to do with litigation. Sometimes, car accident cases cannot be settled, and going to trial is inevitable. At that point, the family care doctor may be subpoenaed to testify or, at the very least, get deposed by the two litigating parties. A family care doctor (generally) is more used to treating colds, stomach pains, and twisted ankles than testifying in open court. Plus, this whole process can be very time consuming for him, and in turn, very costly because he or she cannot see their usual patients.
