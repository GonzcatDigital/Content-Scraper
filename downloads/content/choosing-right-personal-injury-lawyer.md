## What to Ask the Right Personal Injury Lawyer?
In your search for the right personal injury lawyer, you’ll need to ask several questions, including:

## Do you provide a free initial consultation as the right personal injury lawyer?

A lawyer who will charge you for an initial consultation might not be the right personal injury lawyer because a consultation will help both of you determine whether you’re a good fit for each other. One who charges an initial fee just for meeting and learning about your potential case might just be interested in money, not in helping you through this difficult time, and not the right personal injury lawyer for you.

## How do you get paid?

Most people who have been injured tend to work with attorneys who get paid on a contingency basis. This means that the lawyer won’t make a dime unless you win your case. That’s good for two reasons: one, your lawyer will be financially motivated to help you win, and two, the attorney will probably not choose to take on your case unless she feels there is a good chance that you _will_ win.

## How much experience do you have in dealing with cases like mine?

Whether you’re suffering from a [traumatic brain injury](https://phxinjurylaw.com/chronic-pain-traumatic-brain-injury-car-accident/) or you’re looking for compensation because of a [defective medical device](https://www.eastonlawoffices.com/blog/how-common-are-defective-medical-devices-2/), you probably want to work with a personal injury lawyer who has represented people in situations similar to yours. That’s not to say that another lawyer can’t represent you successfully, but it is nice to have someone who has experience in the same type of case. That goes for people whose children have suffered birth injuries, people who have been in car accidents, and people who have fallen and gotten hurt, as well.

It’s also essential that you work with a personal injury lawyer who “gets” you—someone who can connect with you on a personal level and who won’t treat your case as just another file.

During your [initial consultation](https://phxinjurylaw.com/contact-us/), it’s okay to ask as many questions as you want. That’s what your lawyer is there for. She’ll actually welcome your questions because, at the same time, she’s trying to determine whether you’ll work well together.
