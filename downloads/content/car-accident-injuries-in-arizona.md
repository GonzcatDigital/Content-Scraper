## The Price of Your Car Accident Injuries in Arizona

![calculating fees for a car accident injuries in Arizona](data:image/svg+xml;nitro-empty-id=MzIwOjQ5OA==-1;base64,PHN2ZyB2aWV3Qm94PSIwIDAgMzAwIDIwMCIgd2lkdGg9IjMwMCIgaGVpZ2h0PSIyMDAiIHhtbG5zPSJodHRwOi8vd3d3LnczLm9yZy8yMDAwL3N2ZyI+PC9zdmc+)Insurance companies will take a colder approach when it comes to evaluating the settlement amount as they will consider how many bodily car accident injuries in Arizona were sustained; the ongoing medical costs, and even where your car accident injuries are. For example, a [broken leg](/assets/images/blog/car-accident-injuries-in-arizona-01.jpg) will have more ‘value’ than a sprained wrist or hand but much less than a [head injury](https://phxinjurylaw.com/practice-areas/car-accident-brain-injury/). Sounds cruel, right? But that’s the sad reality when it comes to how insurance companies will attribute value to every case they handle. 

If you’re making a claim against someone else’s insurance company, they’re more likely to be extra vigilant when considering your injuries. After your medical assessment is shared, the insurance company will have started looking at your injuries listed and [attributing a cost to your overall claim.](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/auto-accident-loans-after-incident/)

Don’t forget- your mental anguish and pain will also be recorded and considered by the insurance company as a separate injury, thanks to recent changes in [Arizona state law](https://www.azleg.gov/arsDetail/?title=28). All of these factors will be considered as ongoing symptoms and will create a better understanding of the insurers when they build your claim.
