## Understanding the Risks of Head On Collisions 

A head-on collision is a crash involving the front of the car. Two vehicles hit each other directly from the front, usually while going in opposite directions. A head-on collision can also occur when a vehicle heads straight into a stationary object, such as a telephone pole or road barrier. In such a case, a **head on collision lawyer** **is vital to help assess the damages** and negotiate with the insurance company for fair compensation.

### Why These Accidents Are Highly Dangerous 

Because the two vehicles are colliding directly, from a physical standpoint, head-on collisions produce a substantial [amount of force](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0001457587900315), much more significant than any other type of crash. Vehicle hoods can deform to absorb some of the force of the crash, but these types of collisions are often among the most dangerous accidents that occur on the roads. It is always a good idea to **consult with a** **head on collision lawyer** since, in many cases, a total loss of the vehicle is the only result, and all possible help will be needed to obtain your compensation.

## Common Causes of Head-On Collisions in Arizona 

According to our head on collision attorney, head-on collisions typically occur when a car crosses into the oncoming lane and collides with another vehicle traveling in the opposite direction. Drivers traveling in the wrong direction on one-way roads or highways can also cause head-on collisions.

There are many possible causes of [these types of collisions](https://phxinjurylaw.com/head-on-collisions/), many of which are similar to those of other traffic accidents. Distracted driving, driving under the influence of alcohol, failure to obey traffic laws, and more. If you’ve been involved in one, a **head on collision lawyer** can help.

In this scenario, the at-fault driver may be liable for injuries and damages. 

### Factors Leading to Head-On Crashes 

However, our head on collision lawyer also mentions that sometimes, external factors can cause a driver to go in the wrong direction. In such cases, someone else may be liable for the accident. Here are some examples of other parties that can cause a head-on collision:

-   **Pedestrians**

A driver may swerve into oncoming traffic to avoid a pedestrian who is jaywalking. When that happens, the pedestrian may be liable for the accident. A head on collision lawyer can help you determine the pedestrian’s fault by conducting the necessary investigations.

-   **A Government Agency**

A government entity can cause accidents if it fails to maintain roads properly or implements poor road designs. Potholes, poorly marked turn lanes, and poorly marked reversible lanes can all cause head-on collisions. A head-on collision lawyer will hold the parties involved accountable.

-   **Manufacturers**

Defective car parts, such as tires and brakes, can cause accidents. The manufacturer of the vehicle or parts can be held liable for accidents caused by these defects. Your head on collision lawyer can help you determine the liability of the manufacturers so that you are covered for damages from your accident.
