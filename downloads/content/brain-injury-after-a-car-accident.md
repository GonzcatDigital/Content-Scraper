# Brain Injury After a DUI Accident

There are many problems that can come from being the victim of a drunk driver, but a [DUI accident](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/dui-laws-in-arizona-explained/) is not just about damage that can be easily seen. There are other issues, such as a [brain injury after a car accident](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/phoenix-brain-injury-lawyers/), that can be harder to detect. But these are just as serious–if not more so–than the obvious physical injuries.

March is brain injury awareness month, and it’s an important time to remind everyone that it’s important to get checked out thoroughly after an accident. If you do have a brain injury, an [Arizona personal injury lawyer](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/a-guide-to-personal-injury-in-arizona/) can help you receive fair compensation.

Living with a brain injury after a car accident can be temporary as the brain can become “bruised” and need time to heal, such as when a person gets a concussion. However, there are more severe, long-lasting problems that can occur when the brain is injured in a serious DUI accident. Brain injury after a car accident can lead to a range of physical, cognitive, and emotional symptoms, and can have a major impact on a person’s quality of life.

If there is permanent injury to the brain, it is possible that the injured party may never recover all of the function he or she had before the injury took place. That can keep the victim from working and can also cause problems in his or her personal life and relationships, including issues as a result from changes in personality.

There are signs and symptoms of brain injury that can appear after a head-on auto collision or a DUI-caused car or bicycle accident. Some of these signs and symptoms may include mild problems such as headache, nausea, and sensitivity to light all the way up through much more serious symptoms such as profound confusion, slurred speech, and convulsions or seizures.
