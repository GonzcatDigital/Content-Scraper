## Arizona Restitution Payments & Recover Damages

In Arizona, victims of drunk drivers can recover damages in the form of Arizona restitution payments from the drunk driver. The process to recover Arizona restitution payment is not automatic, but in some cases, the outcome can mean well-deserved relief to victims covering a [wide variety of losses](https://www.ncjrs.gov/ovc_archives/bulletins/legalseries/bulletin6/2.html). Courts across the country have authorized restitution against a drunk driver to cover losses due to medical expenses, [lost wages](https://phxinjurylaw.com/blog/lost-wages-vs-lost-earning-capacity/), counseling expenses, property damage, and funeral expenses. The Arizona Court of Appeals has interpreted its state statute to allow a victim’s family to recover damages for wages that were lost due to any care-taking duties that the drunk driver caused due to the accident.

In order to [recover restitution](https://www.azag.gov/criminal/victim-services/victim-compensation-restitution), it is advised that a victim make their request known as soon as practicable so that the court has all the information needed to make a decision during the drunk driver’s sentencing. Once a court has ordered a drunk driver to pay restitution, the money owed to the victim will generally pay to the court clerk’s office and then be distributed to the victim. Courts typically view restitution payments as an important part of the process and so will sometimes allow [online payments](https://prodpci.etimspayments.com/pbw/include/arizona/input.jsp) to help victims obtain their money faster.
